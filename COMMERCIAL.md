# Commercial & enterprise licensing

Bharga Mail is **free and open source under the [GNU AGPL‑3.0](LICENSE)**.

- **Individuals & self‑hosters:** use it freely, forever, under the AGPL. Bring your own AI key — there's nothing to pay.
- **Companies & public sector:** you *may* run the AGPL version too. Most organisations, however, want one or more of the things below — and that's what we offer commercially.

## When you need a commercial agreement

The AGPL requires that if you modify Bharga Mail and offer it to others over a network, you publish your changes under the AGPL. If that doesn't fit your organisation — or you simply need a vendor relationship — talk to us.

## What we offer organisations

- **Commercial license** — use and modify Bharga Mail without AGPL copyleft obligations.
- **Support & SLA** — a named security contact, guaranteed response times, and a supported, signed build (required by most enterprise & public‑sector IT).
- **GDPR / compliance pack** — data‑processing documentation, BSI‑aligned hardening notes, and security review support.
- **Enterprise features** — SSO / SAML, central administration & policy enforcement, audit logging.
- **On‑prem encrypted sync server** — keep multi‑device sync inside your own infrastructure (zero‑knowledge; we never see plaintext mail).
- **Custom work** — domain integrations, branding, and onboarding.

## Why "Made in Germany" matters here

Bharga Mail is local‑first: mail and credentials stay on the device, not on a vendor's servers. For German and EU organisations pursuing **digital sovereignty**, that — plus open, auditable code — is the point. We help you deploy it in a way your DPO and procurement can sign off on.
